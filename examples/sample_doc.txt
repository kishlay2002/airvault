Data Retention and Classification Policy
Version 2.1 — Effective January 2025

1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for retaining and classifying company documents
to ensure regulatory compliance and operational efficiency.

2. Document Retention Periods
All classified documents must be retained for a minimum of 7 years from the date
of creation. Internal documents must be reviewed annually for potential
reclassification. Public-facing documents have no mandatory retention period but
should be archived for reference.

3. Classification Levels
Documents are classified into four sensitivity tiers:
- Public: Information approved for external distribution
- Internal: Day-to-day business information not meant for public release
- Confidential: Sensitive business information including financial projections,
  merger discussions, and trade secrets
- Restricted: Highly sensitive material requiring explicit need-to-know access

4. Employee Responsibilities
All employees must classify documents at the time of creation. Misclassification
may result in disciplinary action. When in doubt, classify at the higher tier
and request review from your department's data steward.

5. Data Destruction
When retention periods expire, documents must be destroyed using approved methods:
digital files via secure deletion (DoD 5220.22-M standard), physical documents
via cross-cut shredding. Destruction must be logged in the compliance system.

6. Annual Review
This policy undergoes annual review by the Chief Information Security Officer.
Next review date: January 2026.
